# Open Training Platform

An open-source platform for managing and delivering SCORM-based training content.

## Vision

Open Training Platform aims to provide a lightweight, standards-compliant alternative to commercial training content platforms. The initial focus is on course content management, course playback, learner progress tracking, and support for learning content standards such as SCORM.

This project is intentionally focused on the training player and content runtime experience rather than full Learning Management System (LMS) functionality.

## Project Goals

- Upload and manage SCORM packages
- Launch and play training content
- Track learner progress and completion
- Support industry learning standards
- Provide a simple deployment model
- Remain open source and community friendly

## Non-Goals

The following are intentionally out of scope for the initial releases:

- Full LMS functionality
- Certifications and credentialing
- Billing and subscriptions
- Marketplace features
- Advanced reporting and analytics

## Current Status

🚧 Early development

The project is currently focused on establishing the foundational capabilities required to upload, launch, and track SCORM content.
